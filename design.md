# BHARDWAJ MARBLES
## Website Design & Product Requirements Document

Version: 1.0

---

# PROJECT OVERVIEW

Build a premium luxury stone and architectural surface website for Bhardwaj Marbles.

The website must position Bhardwaj Marbles as a premium stone craftsmanship company rather than a traditional marble retailer.

The website should attract:

1. Architects
2. Interior Designers
3. Builders
4. Luxury Home Owners
5. Commercial Project Owners
6. Hotels & Hospitality Projects
7. General Residential Customers

The goal is to showcase expertise, craftsmanship, materials, and completed work while generating phone inquiries and WhatsApp leads.

---
